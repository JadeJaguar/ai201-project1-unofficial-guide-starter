Q2: What is the grace period for an F-1 student after they complete their program?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
The 60-day "grace" period after graduation or post-completion OPT is for an F-1 student to prepare to leave the United States, unless the student is beginning a new program of study.

Q3: What are the requirements to qualify for the STEM OPT 24-month extension?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
To qualify for the STEM OPT 24-month extension, the requirements are: 

1. Maintain valid F-1 status
2. Be in a valid period of post-completion OPT
3. Have a degree in a field listed on the STEM Designated Degree Program List
4. Have an offer of employment from an employer enrolled in the E-Verify program
5. Have employment that is directly related to your STEM degree field
6. Have a Form I-983 Training Plan completed and signed by you and your STEM OPT employer

Additionally, if the STEM degree was previously obtained, the following requirements apply:
1. Both degrees were received from accredited and SEVP-certified institutions in the United States
2. The STEM-eligible degree was conferred within 10 years of the STEM OPT extension application
3. You haven't already been granted a STEM OPT extension based on the previous degree
4. Your employment is directly related to the STEM degree.

SOURCES:
  - University ISO guide (Dartmouth), Work Auth
  - Student blog/3rd-party (StudentSucceed), Work Auth

Q5: What is the total number of unemployment days allowed across regular OPT and the STEM OPT extension combined, and how is that split between regular OPT and the STEM extension?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
The total number of unemployment days allowed is 150 days for the entire OPT period, including both the initial period of post-completion OPT and the STEM OPT extension period. The breakdown of this total between regular OPT and the STEM OPT extension is not specified.

F2: According to the university employment options handout, what are the limitations on using CPT, and can using full-time CPT affect my OPT eligibility?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
According to the passages, the limitations on using CPT include: 
1. You must have a job offer before applying for CPT employment.
2. The work must be directly related to your field of study.
3. You must have been enrolled in school full-time for a year under valid F-1 status.
4. You can only work for the specific employer and for the specific dates authorized.
5. While in school, you can only be approved for part-time CPT (20 hours per week or less).

Using full-time CPT for 12 months or more can affect your OPT eligibility, making you ineligible for OPT. However, working part-time on CPT or full-time on CPT for less than 12 months does not affect your OPT eligibility.

SOURCES:
  - Student blog/3rd-party (InternationalStudent.com), Work Auth
  - Student blog/3rd-party (InternationalStudentLoan.com), Work Auth
