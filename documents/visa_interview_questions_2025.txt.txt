F1 Student Visa in US Also known as the US Student visa, this visa is given to those international students who are enrolled in full-time courses in US universities. It is one of the most popular visas in the United States that students doing both undergraduate and postgraduate studies get. However, obtaining this visa is not an easy task—students must undergo a rigorous application process, successfully pass an interview, and have their F1 student visa approved before they can begin their studies in the USA.

Eligibility Requirements For F1 Student Visa US 2025 It is mandatory for students to meet the following eligibility criteria in order to be eligible for an F1 student visa in 2025: You need to prove your intention of returning to your home country after completing the studies, failing which your visa application will be rejected. Ensure that the US university that you have applied to has accepted your application. Everything that you have must show that you are connected to your home country.

Documents Required For Appearing In The F1 Students Visa Interview It is highly necessary to fulfill the US student visa requirements to successfully attain the F1 student visa. Prepare the following documents to match the US student visa requirements. Personal documents: A valid passport, visa application fee receipt, visa interview appointment letter, DS-160 form confirmation slip with clear barcode details, SEVIS fee receipt, and 2*2 inch photographs taken within the last six months. Academic transcripts: academic transcripts of classes 10th and 12th, along with mark sheets, GRE, GMAT or SAT test scores, and English language proficiency tests like IELTS or TOEFL scores, and a consolidated mark sheet of the previous study level; if not, then a provisional certificate. Work or job proofs: joining letter, resignation acceptance letter and salary slips of the previous six months. Financial documents: educational loan approval letter, if any; last three years of bank statements; fixed deposit certificates; tax returns; and proof of property.
