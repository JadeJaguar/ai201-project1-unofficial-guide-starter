It's crucial to adhere to the regulations established by the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) regarding international travel as an F1 student. Generally, F1 students are permitted to travel outside the United States during their academic program, including throughout the semester. However, there are several factors to consider:

Maintaining Status: To uphold your F1 status, you must ensure that you are enrolled in a full course of study and making satisfactory academic progress. Traveling during the semester should not disrupt your ability to attend classes and complete coursework.

Valid I-20 and Visa: Confirm that your Form I-20 is valid and signed by an authorized Designated School Official (DSO) within the last 12 months. Additionally, verify that your F1 visa remains valid for re-entry into the United States.

Re-entry to the U.S.: Upon re-entering the U.S., you will need to present your valid passport, valid F1 visa, and the I-20 signed for travel. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) officers at the port of entry will assess your admissibility.
