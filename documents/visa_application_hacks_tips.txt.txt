Step-by-Step US Student Visa Process Before diving into US visa hacks for international students, let’s review how to apply for a US student visa. Step 1: Get admission in a SEVP-approved US university. Step 2: After your admission is confirmed, you will receive form I-20. Step 3: Pay the SEVIS fees of USD 350. Step 4: Next, fill up the DS-160 form carefully and submit it online. Step 5: The next step is to schedule your US F-1 visa interview at your nearest US consulate. Step 6: Keep documents required for US student visa like academic transcripts, proof of finance, I-20, a valid passport, and DS-160 confirmation handy. Step 7: Give the interview confidently.


How to Prepare for US Student Visa Interview? The best way to get US student visa approved is by clearing the interview. How to pass F1 visa interview questions? Keep the documents hand with you and know your program and applied university thoroughly. Talk about future employment plans in your country, demonstrating a clear intention of returning to your country after your studies get completed. Establish ties with your home country by highlighting your family businesses or properties. Don’t talk about permanent residency in USA. Conduct mock interviews and understand how to convince visa officers for F-1 visa. Don’t reschedule the interview if there is no emergency. Be honest about visa history and spin the reasons for visa rejection in a positive manner, if any.

Top 10 F-1 Visa Approval Hacks for Students. 
1. Apply at least 8-10 months before your admission intake. 
2. Choose spring or fall intakes and don’t delay in booking the slots as soon as you receive I-20. 
3. Cross verify every section of your DS-160 form and check whether you have used capitals wherever required or not. Also, fill up even the optional fields. 
4. Ensure you have enough proof of funds for US student visa to demonstrate that you are capable of funding at least the first year costs of study and living in the US. If you are an undergraduate student, you must have a minimum of 50,000 USD, and if you want to enrol in graduate programs, then you must have a minimum of 60,000 USD. You must also have a grant or assistantship letter if you have enrolled in STEM PhDs. 
5. Stay updated on US visa policy changes and keep all receipts and confirmation mails with you.
