This guide covers F-1 student visa knowledge for international students
 in the US, including visa applications, maintaining status, 
 work authorization like OPT, CPT, and STEM OPT, travel, and 
 practical tips like taxes and credit history. Official sources 
 like USCIS and university websites have the rules, but they 
 leave out the stuff students actually tell each other on 
 Reddit and forums. This project puts both together so you can 
 just ask a question and get a pretty good answer.

Always verify with official US gov websites for up-to date policies
